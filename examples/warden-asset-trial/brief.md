# Verdigris Watch — procedural asset trial

Create a compact, original fantasy RPG asset family using Rtistree's existing 2D
studio. This is a trial for user assessment, not an approved production library.
No generated imagery, downloaded pixels, 3D models, or renderer changes.

Deliver an armoured woodland sentinel in watch, stride and defensive poses, an
eight-frame walk loop, and a sword, shield, axe, lantern, coffer and potion.
Transparent PNGs, consistent foot pivots, reusable equipment drawing functions,
an atlas with frame metadata, saved recipes and a local inspection viewer.

Visual direction: illustrated game sprites with restrained dark contours,
cool bevelled steel, aged brass, evergreen cloth and amber accents. Light comes
from upper left. Forms should remain readable at 128–160 pixels tall.

Reference: https://x.com/Dimillian/status/2097587442538738110
Observed directly: an armoured character shown in multiple facing/pose cells,
within an asset browsing tool. The author describes procedural code and refinement
tools. This informs the deliverable and inspection workflow, not a claim that the
trial reproduces that engine, animation system, or quality.

Construction reference: the local rig below is an authored design study, not a
measured anatomical reference. Use a deliberately stylised five-head figure.
Pelvis supports paired hip–knee–ankle chains; shoulder–elbow–wrist chains carry
equipment. Chest overlaps pelvis, gorget supports helmet, plates overlap cloth.
Equipment attaches to wrist landmarks, and shield and sword shapes are shared
with the standalone icons. Avoid opaque equipment hiding every limb connection.

Foundation criteria, fixed before drawing:

- Three meaningfully different poses: settled watch, split stride, raised guard.
- Separate head, sword, shield and both feet at thumbnail size.
- Continuous limb connections and plausible planted balance for stylised sprites.
- Grayscale hierarchy separates helmet, torso, equipment and receding cloak.

Inspect silhouette/value sheets before finish. Preserve a before/after if a
structural defect appears. Technical replay is assessed separately from taste.
Final quality remains awaiting the user's review. The walk is a limited 2D rig
experiment; do not claim eight facing directions or production animation.
