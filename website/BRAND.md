# Rtistree identity

The name comes from artistry, drops the “a”, and deliberately contains “tree”.
The identity joins expressive artwork with the connected structure that makes it editable.

- Use the shared `Brand.astro` component on the homepage, examples and documentation.
- The branching glyph is an abstract tree of connected nodes. It is also the favicon.
- Set the lowercase wordmark in DM Serif Display; distinguish `tree` in italic and the accent colour.
- Preserve the charcoal, forest-green and lime palette. Inter remains the reading and interface typeface.
- Use expressive serif headlines with plain, precise supporting copy. “Artistry. Rooted in code.” is the primary line.
- Let branches and roots explain real features: scene hierarchy, shared source and focused edits. Avoid unrelated nature imagery and excessive growth metaphors.
- Keep the glyph visible at small sizes, with clear space around it and a contrasting background.
- Font licenses are copied alongside the served font files during content synchronization.

`public/brand/mark.svg` is the editable glyph source. Keep favicon geometry aligned with it.
